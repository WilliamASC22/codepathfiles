Changemakers Expo

Spotlighting the organizations driving transformation

About the Event

This event is designed to showcase a the organizations that are making an impact in the lives of many people.

Each organization will have its own booth where attendees can learn about the organizations mission, hear real stories of their visions for the future, and even donate directly to the organizations and support them.

This event is open to anyone who is interested in giving to those in need, learning more about nonprofit organizations, or discovering new ways to support their community and people around the world.

The aim of this event is to connect individual that are interested in philanthropy with organizations that are philanthropic.

What to Expect

Learn more about the charities that make a difference.

  • HopeBridge Foundation: Dedicated to global humanitarian aid.
  • NextGen Scholars: Empowering underserved youth.
  • GreenSpark Initiative: Focused on sustainable energy.
  • Lifeline Horizon: Providing healthcare in rural communities.
  • ShelterSeed: Building affordable housing.
  • BrightPath Alliance: Connecting communities to digital access.
  • Books for Brighter Days: Distributing books around the world.
  • WellSpring Outreach: Delivering clean water.
  • Clean Earth Collective: Promoting recycling.
  • NeighborFirst Network: Supporting local volunteers.
